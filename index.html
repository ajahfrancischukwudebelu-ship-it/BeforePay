FAQ

Questions, answered.

Does a low-risk result mean the seller is safe?

No. A screening can miss fraud. Always verify independently.

Does BeforePay guarantee refunds?

No. BeforePay is an educational screening tool, not insurance or escrow.

What should I never submit?

Passwords, card numbers, OTPs, gift-card redemption codes, private keys and other secrets.

How can this become a real business?

Future versions can add public-web checks, verified business profiles, paid detailed reports and business subscriptions.
